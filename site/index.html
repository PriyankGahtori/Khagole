What it computes

        

          

            
🪔 Vedic Kundli & Divisional Vargas

            
Computes D1 (Rashi), D9 (Navamsha), and all 16 Shodashavarga charts (D2 to D60). Rendered in high-contrast North Indian (diamond) and South Indian (box) SVGs with Kendra fills and Lagna highlights.

          

          

            
⏳ Vimshottari Dasha (Canonical TSSY)

            
Multi-level Mahadasha, Antardasha, and Pratyantardasha progressions. Supports True Sidereal Solar Year (TSSY) solar return solver matching canonical Parashari standards, alongside Sidereal, Savana, and Tropical years.

          

          

            
☀️ Panchanga (Five Limbs of Time)

            
Calculates Tithi, Vara, Nakshatra with Pada, Yoga, and Karana in sub-millisecond execution directly from high-precision Sun and Moon longitudes.

          

          

            
🔍 Global City Search & Auto-Timezone

            
Search any city across the globe with instant autocomplete geocoding. Automatically derives latitude, longitude, and historical/daylight-saving UTC timezone offsets.

          

          

            
🪐 Navagrahas & Lagna Precision

            
Surya to Ketu, outer planets, with instantaneous daily speed and retrograde status (Vakri), and exact Ascendant (Lagna) degrees from JPL DE440s ephemerides.

          

          

            
🏛️ Vedic House Systems

            
Whole Sign (Rashi Bhava) standard Parashari, traditional Sripati (Bhava Chalita), Equal House, and cuspal systems with exact planet-to-house mappings.

          

          

            
☸️ Canonical Vedic Ayanamsas

            
Supports Chitrapaksha Lahiri (standard Vedic default), True Chitra / Invariable Plane, B.V. Raman, Krishnamurti (KP), Sri Yukteswar, and Fagan-Bradley.

          

          

            
📐 Classical Parashari Standards

            
Mean nodes (Rahu/Ketu strictly 180° opposite), geometric true ephemeris positions, and exact Moon-longitude Mahadasha balance at birth.

          

        

      

    


    
    

      

        
About AstroCeleste Engine

        

          

            
Canonical Parashari Calibration

            
Defaults are strictly aligned with canonical Vedic Parashari standards: Sidereal zodiac, Lahiri ayanamsa, Mean nodes (TrueNodes=0), Geometric positions (TruePositions=1), Whole Sign houses (W), and TSSY Dasha year standard.

          

          

            
100% In-Browser Privacy

            
Pure Rust WebAssembly compiles with zero C dependencies. Ephemeris lookups, astrological reductions, and chart generations occur entirely on your local machine with no user data ever sent to remote servers.

          

          

            
NASA JPL Ephemerides

            
Sub-arcsecond celestial positions computed directly from NASA JPL DE440s (1849–2150) or DE441 (13200 BC–17191 AD) Chebyshev polynomials with IAU 2006 precession and IAU 2000A nutation.

Verified, not approximated

        

          

            
JPL ephemerides

            
Positions come from NASA JPL DE440s (1849–2150) or DE441 (13200 BC–17191 AD), read by a pure-Rust SPK reader. A date outside the loaded kernels is an error, never a guess.

          

          

            
277 golden charts

            
Every release reproduces the reference implementation's output for natal, horary, transit, synastry and derived charts: floats within 10−9, with the same keys in the same order.

          

          

            
Checked stage by stage

            
Time scales, precession, nutation, light-time, deflection and aberration are checked against Skyfield, and raw kernel states against jplephem.

          

        

        

          Pure Rust with no C code and two dependencies (serde, serde_json). Builds for servers,
          wasm32-unknown-unknown, Android and iOS.
